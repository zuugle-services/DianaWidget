The trip at a glance.

Into the ice kingdom.

The Marktschellenberger Eishöhle is Germany's largest ice cave, set deep in the Untersberg massif. Inside wait glittering ice formations, frozen waterfalls and vast caverns, shaped over thousands of years by ice and rock.

Getting there is part of the adventure: the cave is reached on foot, and the planner finds the public transport connections to the trailhead and back home again, timed to the tour.

Cave highlights.

  • Ice formations

    Ice columns, frozen falls and caverns lit for the tour.

  • Germany's largest

    The biggest ice cave in the country, inside the Untersberg.

  • Guided tours

    Visits run as guided tours of about an hour.

  • Around 0 °C

    Constantly near freezing inside, even in high summer.

What to bring.

  • Warm layers: it is near freezing inside
  • Sturdy hiking boots with good grip
  • Gloves and a warm hat
  • A headlamp, though tours are lit
  • A small backpack with water and snacks
  • Sunscreen and sunglasses for the hike

Two days on the Rax.

        
The Rax massif is the classic mountain getaway from Vienna: alpine meadows on the plateau, wide views over the surrounding ranges and a choice of trails, from gentle paths to demanding ascents.

        
This route takes two days, with a night in a traditional mountain hut. The planner treats it as one trip: the connection there on the first day and the way home on the second, using realtime data.
